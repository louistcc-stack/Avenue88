FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Do I pay anything for the first chat or price assessment?

No. The first chat and your price range are free and there's no obligation.

Can you help me sell my HDB and buy a condo at the same time?

Yes, HDB upgrading is one of our main specialities. We plan the timeline so the sale and purchase line up.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Singapore. Some of us focus on D9, 10, 11 and D15 East Coast, so we'll match you with the right person.

What's your commission?

Our commission starts from 1%. The final rate depends on your property type and the services you need, and it's always agreed with you in writing upfront. Chat with us for a no-obligation quote.
