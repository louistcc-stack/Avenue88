    Questions? Fair enough.

    What does PROPERTY stand for?
    • PProximity: how close it is to the MRT
    • RRemaining Lease: years left on the lease
    • OOwner Upgraders: the nearby HDB upgrader pool
    • PPrimary Schools: access to popular schools
    • EEstate Growth: URA growth areas
    • RRelease of Land: upcoming land sales nearby
    • TTotal Units: project size
    • YYield: area rental yield

    Together, these eight make up the sides of your Octa Score.

    Is the Octa Score really free?

    Yes. There's no charge and no obligation to buy or sell through us.

    What kinds of homes can you score?

    Private homes in Singapore: condos, new launches, ECs and landed homes. The Octa Score isn't designed for HDB flats.

    Is this a valuation?

    No. It's an indicative guide to help you compare homes. For a formal valuation, you'd need a licensed valuer.

    How long does it take?

    We'll get back to you to confirm the details, then go through your score with you.

    Can you compare two homes?

    Sure. Just put both in the form and we'll score them side by side.
